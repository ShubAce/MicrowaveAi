1-36: Punjabi Mathi

  • Weight Limit: 5 pcs

  • Utensil: Air fry tray

Ingredients

List

Weight

For Punjabi Mathi

Weight Limit

5 pcs

Maida

1 cup

Semolina

0.25 cup

Carom seeds

0.5 cup

Kasuri Methi

1 tsp

Black peppercorn (crushed)

0.5 tbsp

Ghee

3 tbsps

Salt

As per taste

Water

0.3 cup

Ghee (for brushing)

As required

Instructions

  1. Take maida, semolina, carom seeds, kasuri methi, black peppercorn (crushed), ghee, and salt in a bowl.

  2. Mix all the ingredients together until the mixture turns crumbly.

  3. Add water and knead into a firm dough.

    • It should be harder than Paratha dough.

  4. Cover with a muslin cloth and set aside for 20 minutes.

  5. Knead the dough again for a minute and divide it into 5 balls. Put them on a rolling board, press them slightly using your palm, and shape them into rounds with around 0.5 cm thickness.

  6. Make pricks on the flattened mathi using a fork.

  7. Take the air fry tray and place the mathi on it. Brush with ghee and place inside the microwave oven. Select the category and press START.

  8. When the microwave oven beeps, take out the food, turn it over, brush it with ghee, put it back inside, and press START.

1-37: Stuffed Aloo Patties

  • Weight Limit: 5 pcs

  • Utensil: Air fry tray

Ingredients

List

Weight

For Stuffed Aloo Patties

Weight Limit

5 pcs

For patties

Boiled and mashed potato

2 cups

Corn flour

1 tbsp

Finely chopped green chilli

2 nos.

Salt

As per taste

Oil (for brushing)

As required

For stuffing

Oil

2 tsps

Boiled green pea

0.5 cup

Ginger and green chilli paste

2 tsps

Chopped coriander leaves

2 tbsps

Cumin seeds

1 tsp

Salt, Red chilli powder, Garam masala

As per taste

Lemon juice (optional)

2 tbsps

Instructions

  1. Pre-preparation for stuffing: In a microwave safe glass bowl, take oil and cumin seeds, and microwave at 100% for 3 minutes. Add boiled green pea, ginger and green chili paste, chopped coriander leaves, and all spices. Mix well and microwave at 100% for 2 minutes. Squeeze fresh lemon juice and mix well.

  2. In a bowl, take boiled and mashed potato, chopped green chili, corn flour, and salt, and mix very well to make a dough for patties.

  3. Divide the dough into 6 equal parts and make round balls.

  4. Stuff each potato ball with the stuffing in the center and seal it. Shape into round patties.

  5. Place all the patties on the air fry tray. Brush with oil on both sides and place inside the microwave oven. Select the category and press START.

Note

  • Serving suggestion: When cooking ends, serve the food with mint chutney or tomato ketchup.

For Mushroom Rolls

For Stuffing

1.5 cups

Onion (chopped)

4 tbsps

Green chilli (chopped)

2 nos.

Butter (melted)

2 tbsps

Fresh cream

4 tbsps

Maida

1 tbsp

Boiled pea

0.5 cup

Lemon juice

1 tsp

Coriander leaves (chopped)

3 tbsps

Garlic paste

0.5 tsp

Salt, Black pepper powder

As per taste

For Rolls

Bread slices (white)

8 nos.

Butter / Oil (for brushing)

As required

Instructions

  1. Pre-Preparation for Stuffing: In a microwave safe glass bowl, take butter, chopped cumin, green chilli, onion, and mushrooms. Mix and microwave at 100% power for 6 minutes. Add boiled peas, chopped coriander, garlic paste, fresh cream, lemon, salt, and black pepper powder. Mix very well and microwave at 100% power for 3 minutes. Cover and set aside.

  2. Trim the sides of the bread slices with a knife. Sprinkle a little water and flatten the bread slices with a rolling pin. Place one portion of the stuffing in the center of each flattened bread slice and roll tightly to cover the mixture. Set aside for 5-7 minutes.

  3. Cut the bread rolls in half. Put them carefully on the air fry tray and brush them with oil. Keep them inside the microwave oven.

  4. Select the category and press START.
