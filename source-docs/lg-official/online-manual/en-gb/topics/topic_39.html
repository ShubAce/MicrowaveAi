As per taste

Instructions

  1. In Microwave safe glass bowl add oil and vegetables, ginger-garlic paste. Select category and press start.

  2. When beeps, add salt, pepper, water and palak (in pieces). Mix well and put in microwave and press start.

    How to make Wonton :Maida -1 cup, Salt - 1 pinch, Oil - 1 tsp

    Method : Mix all the ingredients in the bowl and make dough with too warm water. After that cover the bowl and leave the mixture for 10-15 minutes.

    For Stuffing :Cabbage, carrot, capsicum which is cut in long pieces

    Method : Mix all the ingredients for stuffing. Make a small puree of wonton and put the small quantity of stuffing in between them and then cover the puree and press it.
