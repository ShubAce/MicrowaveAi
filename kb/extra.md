# EXTRA FACTS (LG product page, photos of this microwave, door label, catalogue, leaflet)

## The control panel as printed on this microwave

The panel is on the right side of the black glass door. The buttons are glass-touch: touch the printed words with the soft part of a finger (not a fingernail), with dry hands.

From top to bottom:
- "ThinQ" logo, then the blue display. It shows the time of day, cooking time, menu codes (like 1-9), "Loc" when Child Lock is on and "End" when cooking is finished.
- Row 1: **Cook Mode** (small print "*Wi-Fi" under it: hold 3 seconds for Wi-Fi), **Charcoal/Indian Cuisine**, **Healthy Heart**.
- Row 2: **Indian Roti Basket/Ghee**, **Air Fry/Low Calorie**, **Kids'/Dairy Delight**.
- The round dial with "−" on the left and "+" on the right (More/Less): turn right for more, left for less.
- Bottom left: **STOP** in red, with "Cancel" and "*Child Lock" printed under it (hold 3 seconds for Child Lock).
- Bottom right: **START**, with "Select/ +30 seconds" printed under it. "*Hold 3 seconds" is printed below.

## Everyday jobs that are inside the auto-cook menus

- Defrost (thaw frozen food): Charcoal/Indian Cuisine → 5 → 5-2 Defrost Veg or 5-3 Defrost Non Veg.
- Keep food warm: Charcoal/Indian Cuisine → 5 → 5-1 Keep Warm.
- Steam cleaning the inside: Charcoal/Indian Cuisine → 5 → 5-16 Steam Clean.
- Re-crisp soggy namkeen, biscuits, bhujia, papad, wafers: Charcoal/Indian Cuisine → 5 → 5-4 Dehumidification. (LG's product page calls this kind of feature "Dehydrate".)
- Making ghee: Indian Roti Basket/Ghee → 2 (Ghee menus 2-1 to 2-4). LG advertises "Ghee in 12 minutes".
- Warming milk: the Pasteurize Milk menus (Kids'/Dairy Delight → 4, codes 4-1 and 4-2) need a Milk Pasteurization Kit that is NOT included with this model (the manual marks it "Not provided"). To simply warm milk, use a wide microwave-safe glass container and the **START/Select** button (30 seconds at full power per press), following the safety rules for liquids: stir before and halfway through, do not overheat, let it stand, and handle the container carefully because hot milk can boil up suddenly.

## Specifications (LG product page)

- Capacity 28 litres. Microwave output 900 W, 5 power levels.
- Power consumption: microwave 1350 W, grill 1200 W, convection 1950 W.
- Turntable 320 mm. Stainless steel cavity. LED cavity light. 230 V, 50 Hz.
- Size 512 × 311 × 488 mm (W × H × D); weight 19.5 kg.
- Not on this model: kitchen timer, turntable on/off, sensor cooking, steam cooking.

## Features explained (LG catalogue)

- Air Fry: the air fry tray has a vented base, so hot air from the convection fan circulates around the food and makes it crisp with very little oil. There are 60 Air Fry menus (Air Fry/Low Calorie → 1).
- Charcoal Lighting Heater: the heater used for tandoori-style charcoal cooking; it has a long 10-year warranty (parts).
- Motorised rotisserie: the rotisserie bar turns the food 360° for even roasting, without turning it by hand.
- Scan to Cook: in the LG ThinQ phone app, scan the barcode on an ITC ready-to-eat meal and the app sends the cooking settings to the oven (40 Scan to Cook recipes). Needs Wi-Fi and the ThinQ app.
